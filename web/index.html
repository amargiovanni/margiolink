03 / A lighter footprint

Know your audience.
Respect their space.

Useful analytics don’t need a dossier. MargioLink keeps the context of a click — country, device, referrer, campaign — and leaves out the raw IP address and tracking cookie.

Read the privacy notice ↗

A fresh start.
Every single day.

Visitor codes change at midnight UTC and differ for each link. Daily unique counts tell you how a link is doing, without a lasting visitor identifier.

04 / A place of your own

Your domain.
Your data.
Your little corner.

Run MargioLink in your own Cloudflare account. One Worker, one D1 database, and a domain with your name on it.

Get started ↗

Self-hosted. MIT licensed. No subscription to us.

  1. 01

    Make yourself at home.

    Clone the repository and create your D1 database. The setup guide walks you through the configuration.

    git clone https://github.com/amargiovanni/margiolink
  2. 02

    Put your name on it.

    Connect a domain, set your admin credentials and hashing secret, then apply the database migrations.

  3. 03

    Send something into the world.

    Deploy, make your first link, and share it. Or try the local demo to explore a dashboard full of sample traffic.
